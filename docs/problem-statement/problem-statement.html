Problem statement

An early-career software engineer in India who wants a job abroad spends hours every week applying to roles they cannot legally take, because job boards don't show which employers will sponsor a work visa.

Who

A final-year student or engineer with 0 to 3 years of experience in India, applying for jobs in Germany, the UK, Canada, Australia or the US.

When it happens

Every application cycle. Boards list roles that need local work rights or won't sponsor, and say so late or not at all, so the candidate reads, tailors and applies anyway.

What it costs them

Hours of tailoring for applications that cannot succeed, silence or rejections that wear down their standing with recruiters, and fees for done-for-you services that apply in bulk.
