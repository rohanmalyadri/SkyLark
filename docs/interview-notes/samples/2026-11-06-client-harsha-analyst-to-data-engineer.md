## What they said

- No time to apply during the week; wants the team to submit after he approves the list.
- Job title still says "analyst" although most of his work is Airflow and dbt pipelines (14 sources, about 30 million rows a day).
- Notice period 60 days, negotiable to 45. Recruiters drop him at that question.
- Wants a daily summary of what was sent, "so I'm not surprised when someone calls". _(paraphrase)_

## One line in their own words

> "My title says analyst, my work says engineer. I need a resume that says what I actually do." _(sample)_

## What surprised us

- He cared more about control (approving the list, daily summary) than about volume.

## Did they agree with our idea?

**Yes.** Chose the managed plan and approved the first tailored resume.

## What we changed because of this conversation

- Daily "what was sent" summary added to the managed plan.
