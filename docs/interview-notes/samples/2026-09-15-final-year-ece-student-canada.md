# Interview (SAMPLE): final-year ECE student looking at Canada and Ireland, 2026-09-15

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 2026-09-15 |
| **Role** | Final-year B.Tech ECE student, switching to software |
| **Where they are, where they want to work** | Vijayawada → Canada or Ireland |
| **How we met** | Video call (Google Meet), introduced by a senior |
| **Who spoke to them** | Abhiram |
| **Length** | 25 minutes |
| **Consent to quote** | Yes, by role only |

## What they said

- Plans to do an MS first; applying for jobs directly "feels impossible without Canadian experience". _(paraphrase)_
- Applies to around 5 jobs a week, mostly through college WhatsApp groups that forward links.
- Parents are against paying "agents" after a cousin lost ₹40,000 to a consultancy that promised a Dubai job.
- Would only pay after seeing results. Asked if we have a free plan.

## One line in their own words

> "Every consultancy says 100% placement. Why should I believe you are different?" _(sample)_

## What surprised us

- Trust, not price, was the first objection. The word "agent" carries a lot of baggage.

## Did they agree with our idea?

**Partly.** Liked the matching, but would not pay up front.

## What we changed because of this conversation

- Kept a free tier for building a profile and seeing matches; nothing is promised about placement anywhere on the site.
