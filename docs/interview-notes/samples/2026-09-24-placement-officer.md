# Interview (SAMPLE): training and placement officer, engineering college, 2026-09-24

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 2026-09-24 |
| **Role** | Training & placement officer, autonomous engineering college |
| **Where they are, where they want to work** | Hyderabad (helps ~900 final-year students a year) |
| **How we met** | In person, placement cell office (appointment through our HoD) |
| **Who spoke to them** | Both |
| **Length** | 30 minutes |
| **Consent to quote** | Yes, by role only |

## What they said

- About 70% of placed students go through campus drives; the rest are "on their own" and that is where most stress is. _(paraphrase)_
- Every year 15–20 students ask about jobs abroad. The cell has no process for it and points them to MS consultancies.
- Concerned about students paying private services: "I have seen students pay ₹50,000 and get fake offer letters."
- Would consider a college tie-up only if there is no fee charged to students through the college.

## One line in their own words

> "If you are sending applications in the student's name, the student must approve every single one." _(sample)_

## What surprised us

- He raised consent for each application before we did.

## Did they agree with our idea?

**Partly.** Agrees the off-campus gap is real; cautious about any paid model.

## What we changed because of this conversation

- Made approval of the shortlist a required step before a specialist submits anything.
