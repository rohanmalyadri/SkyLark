# Interview (SAMPLE): HR manager at a product startup, 2026-10-08

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 2026-10-08 |
| **Role** | HR and talent acquisition manager, 60-person SaaS startup |
| **Where they are, where they want to work** | Pune |
| **How we met** | Video call (Zoom), via a college alumni group |
| **Who spoke to them** | Rohan |
| **Length** | 25 minutes |
| **Consent to quote** | Yes, by role only |

## What they said

- Hires mostly through referrals and their careers page; job boards bring "volume, not quality". _(paraphrase)_
- GitHub links help only if the README explains what the candidate built. Empty profiles hurt.
- Freshers from service backgrounds often undersell themselves; she sees good people rejected by keyword filters.
- Would not pay for candidates through us; that is not her budget.

## One line in their own words

> "Show me one project properly explained and I'll call you. Don't show me fifteen certificates." _(sample)_

## What surprised us

- She treated certificates as almost negative signal.

## Did they agree with our idea?

**Partly.** Liked better-matched candidates; not a buyer.

## What we changed because of this conversation

- Projects with explained outcomes now count for more than certificates in the profile strength score; GitHub stays evidence only, never a verified credential.
