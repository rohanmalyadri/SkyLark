## What they said

- Applies on weekends only, 10–15 applications. Weekdays are "client calls till 10 pm".
- Uses the IND public register of recognised sponsors to check companies, but says most people don't know it exists.
- Tried an auto-apply Chrome extension: sent 200 applications in two days, got "zero responses and one angry recruiter email" about a wrong cover letter.
- Spent around ₹12,000 on a Udemy bundle and a LinkedIn Premium subscription.

## One line in their own words

> "The bot applied to a Senior Staff role with my two years. That's when I uninstalled it." _(sample)_

## What surprised us

- He already did the sponsor check by hand; he wanted it done for him, not explained to him.

## Did they agree with our idea?

**Partly.** Yes to matching and review; worried a specialist would be slower than doing it himself.

## What we changed because of this conversation

- Seniority mismatch became a hard filter in the matching engine instead of a soft penalty.
