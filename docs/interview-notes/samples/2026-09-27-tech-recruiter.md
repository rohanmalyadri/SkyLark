# Interview (SAMPLE): technical recruiter at a staffing firm, 2026-09-27

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 2026-09-27 |
| **Role** | Technical recruiter, 5 years, IT staffing firm hiring for EU and Indian clients |
| **Where they are, where they want to work** | Bengaluru |
| **How we met** | LinkedIn message, then a call |
| **Who spoke to them** | Abhiram |
| **Length** | 20 minutes |
| **Consent to quote** | Yes, with first name (Priya, fictional) |

## What they said

- Gets 300–400 applications for one Java opening; she screens for under 30 seconds each.
- Can tell mass-applied resumes instantly: same summary, wrong company name, keywords pasted from the JD.
- Would rather see 20 relevant candidates than 400. "Your product helps me only if it sends fewer, better people." _(paraphrase)_
- Notice period and current CTC are the first two things she checks, before skills.

## One line in their own words

> "Auto-apply tools have made my job worse, not easier." _(sample)_

## What surprised us

- Notice period mattered more to her than any skill keyword.

## Did they agree with our idea?

**No, at first; partly by the end.** Against automation; came around once she heard every application is reviewed by a person.

## What we changed because of this conversation

- Added notice period and expected CTC to the profile, and capped weekly applications per candidate.
